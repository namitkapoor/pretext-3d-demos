Pretext × Three.js

3D Text Demos

Experiments that take Pretext's DOM-free line layout and push it into 3D space — words as physics particles, text on canvas textures wrapped over meshes, cloth simulation, and a couple of standalone Three.js studies.

Interactive Card

Word clusters as physics-based UI on a 3D surface. Nav items, headline, and body are independent spring clusters — hover lifts, click scatters, layout is the attractor.

Three Words

Words as interactive 3D particles — Pretext positions become spring attractors in Three.js space. Hover to lift, click to scatter.

Three Texture

Pretext text layout rendered onto a live canvas texture mapped to a rotating 3D card. Drag the width slider to reflow in real time.

Cloth Physics

Pretext text as a canvas texture on an Ammo.js soft body cloth. The cloth hangs from a fixed top edge and ripples under oscillating wind forces.

Pola Mother's Day

Recreation of the Pola "We Care More" site — Three.js instanced bouquet with per-instance sine-wave float, scroll-driven camera, mouse parallax, and IntersectionObserver text reveal.
