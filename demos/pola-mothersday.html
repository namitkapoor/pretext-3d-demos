We Care More Thank you to all mothers.

Mom, thank you. Today, those feelings flow across the whole world. We know there are people who cannot wholeheartedly celebrate Mother's Day from their circumstances. We're sorry. Even so — just once — we want to say it.
The shape of "mother" is not fixed. Whether or not there's a blood connection — more than that, a mother doesn't even need to be a woman. As long as there is love. Everyone who pours all the love they have into someone — that is what a mother is.
POLA has many mothers working here too. Of course, there are those who chose not to become mothers. And those who wanted to, but couldn't. Those diverse lives and values are an irreplaceable treasure. We believe this deeply.
What can POLA do for mothers everywhere? Creating spaces where all kinds of mothers can thrive. Extending our benefits to common-law and same-sex partnerships. Subsidizing fertility treatments and egg-freezing consultations. And for mothers who carry an especially heavy load in raising children — some of our staff have begun volunteering their skills as estheticians.
How many shapes of love exist in that single word, "mother"? This I know for certain: every single one is precious. Today is Mother's Day. I am so glad you are with me. Thank you to all mothers.
